Our website is informational. It has no accounts, sales or payments. We only process the data you send us and the data that is strictly necessary for the website to work.

| Category | Data | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Identity | Full name | Contact form, e-mail |
| Contact | E-mail address; phone number and job title if included in your e-mail signature | Contact form, e-mail |
| Professional | Your organization (optional) | Contact form |
| Request content | What you write to us in the form or by e-mail | Contact form, e-mail |
| Security | IP address, browser and device information, request time | Automatic logs of our hosting and security infrastructure |

We do not ask for special categories of personal data (such as health, biometric data, religion or political opinions). Please do not include such data, or personal data of other people, in the form or in your e-mails.

## How do we collect your data?

Your data is collected electronically when you fill in the contact form, when you send us an e-mail, and through the automatic logs of our hosting infrastructure when you visit the website.

## Who do we share your data with?

We do not sell your personal data or share it for advertising. We may share it only for the purposes above, with:

- **Infrastructure providers.** Cloudflare, Inc. for hosting, content delivery, security and routing e-mails sent to our domain; Web3Forms for delivering contact form submissions to us by e-mail; and our e-mail service provider for hosting our company mailboxes.
- **Competent public authorities.** Only when legally required and limited to the request.
- **Legal and financial advisors.** As needed for our legal obligations and possible disputes.

### International transfers

The servers of the infrastructure providers above may be located outside Türkiye, mainly in the United States and European Union countries. Using these services therefore means that your personal data is transferred abroad. Such transfers are carried out in line with the conditions and procedures set out in Article 9 of the KVKK.

## How long do we keep your data?

| Data | Retention period |
|---|---|

## Cookies and browser storage

Our website does not use advertising, analytics or tracking cookies and has no tool that measures visitor behaviour.

To remember your preferences, only two items are kept in your browser's local storage (localStorage):

| Name | Content | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| `aule-theme` | Dark or light theme | Keeping your chosen theme on your next visit |
| `aule-lang` | Turkish or English | Keeping your chosen language on your next visit |

These items stay on your device, are never sent to us and are not used to identify you. You can delete them at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

Our infrastructure provider Cloudflare may set strictly necessary security cookies to protect the website and distinguish automated attacks. These cookies are not used for marketing or profiling.

If we start using cookies for analytics or similar purposes in the future, we will update this notice and, where required, ask for your consent before using them.

## How do we protect your data?

All connections to the website are encrypted (HTTPS). Your requests are only visible to the people responsible for answering them, and our accounts are protected with strong authentication. We do not copy your data more than needed and keep it only where the purpose requires.

## Data processed in client projects

This notice does not cover data processed in the software we build for our clients. In those projects the client is the data controller and Aule Software acts as a data processor on the client's instructions. These obligations are set out separately in our contracts with clients. We do not feed our clients' real data into AI assisted development tools.

## Your rights

Under Article 11 of the KVKK, you may contact us to:

- Learn whether your personal data is processed,
- Request information about the processing,
- Learn the purpose of the processing and whether the data is used accordingly,
- Know the third parties in Türkiye or abroad to whom your data is transferred,
- Request correction of incomplete or inaccurate data,
- Request deletion or destruction of your data under the conditions of Article 7 of the KVKK,
- Request that corrections, deletions and destructions be notified to the third parties to whom the data was transferred,
- Object to a result against you arising from analysis of your data exclusively by automated systems,
- Claim compensation for damage caused by unlawful processing.

## How to contact us

Under the Communiqué on the Procedures and Principles of Application to the Data Controller, you can send your request:

- In writing to our address above, or through a notary public,
- By registered electronic mail (KEP), secure electronic signature or mobile signature,
- From the e-mail address you previously shared with us and that is registered in our records, to [info@aulesoftware.com](mailto:info@aulesoftware.com).

Your request should include your full name, your signature (for written requests), your address or e-mail address for notifications, and the subject of your request.

If your request is rejected, if you find our answer insufficient, or if we do not respond in time, you may file a complaint with the Personal Data Protection Board.
